## Additional scoped checks

The search also checked exact finite mixed-qubit estimation by Vidal, Latorre, Pascual and Tarrach, [quant-ph/9812068](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9812068); finite mixed-qubit discrimination, [Conlon et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-023-01454-z); and numerical locally unbiased risk bounds, [Yamagata–Ikeura, arXiv:2611.19814](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19814). These differ in source family, loss, or local-unbiasedness requirement. They support the narrow scope of the novelty statement rather than a broad claim about the origin of finite collective advantage. For the last source, the indexed primary abstract was inspected; full-text retrieval was unavailable.

The scope check is not an exhaustive priority certification. All theorem claims made by this repository require its own stated derivations and verification, irrespective of whether a related source was found.
