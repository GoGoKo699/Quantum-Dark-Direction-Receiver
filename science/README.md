# Scientific route

The source hides one ray by uniformly occupying its orthogonal complement. The ideal decision problem asks for the least worst-case infidelity at a fixed specimen budget. The qutrit hardware accounts then charge control errors, storage and reference preparations.

1. [Task and comparisons](model.md) gives the source promises, ray loss and measurement classes.
2. [Exact collective minimax risk](collective-minimax.md) solves every specimen budget and gives the first possible advantage in each dimension of at least three.
3. [Three-qutrit hierarchy](measurement-hierarchy.md) compares full, effectwise biseparable, fixed-cut, sequential pair and adaptive single-copy measurements.
4. [Finite-budget certificates](finite-budget.md) derive the two-specimen tie, exhibit pair receivers and certify the adaptive single-copy lower bound.
5. [Receiver](receiver.md) explains parity, the conjugate hole and the finite 24- and 450-outcome constructions.
6. [Finite implementation](implementation.md) gives randomized settings, bounded precision and physical dilation resources.
7. [Global single-copy converse](one-copy-bound.md) bounds arbitrary adaptive histories at any budget.
8. [Imperfect storage](memory-noise.md) gives the global matrix estimator and its unconditional finite risk.
9. [Paid calibration](calibration.md) counts independent references, rejection and false certification.
10. [Limits](limits.md) gives the coherent exchange ambiguity and finite entanglement-breaking simulation.

The [prior-work comparison](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) credits inherited results. The [verification guide](../verification/README.md) lists the exact certificates and numerical checks.
